        Without sync, everything is stored only in this browser. It will not follow you to another device, and clearing site data erases it.

        Private by design. Your clients and hours stay on your device. With sync on, they are encrypted before they leave it, so this server cannot read them. No account, no ads, no analytics. How?
        • Without sync, nothing you type is sent anywhere. It is stored only in this browser.
        • With sync, your passphrase becomes two keys on your device. One lets the server find your encrypted copy. The other, the only key that can decrypt it, never leaves your device. The server stores scrambled bytes it has no way to read, and never sees your passphrase.
        • Transfer links and QR codes carry their data after the # in the link, a part browsers never send to any server.
        • Email opens your own mail app. Billable does not send anything itself.
        • No outside scripts can run on this page. Your browser enforces that.
        • No IP addresses are recorded by Billable's server. It holds them in memory for up to an hour, only to stop abuse of sync.
        • What is still seen: Cloudflare, which carries the traffic, sees standard request details like any website: your IP address, browser type and when you visit. With sync on, the server sees when an encrypted copy is saved and how big it is.
